# Sales Process

Version: 1.0

---

# Objective

Provide a consistent process from the first customer contact to project delivery.

---

# Sales Lifecycle

1. Discovery Call
2. Infrastructure Assessment
3. Executive Proposal
4. Contract Approval
5. Project Kickoff
6. Project Delivery
7. Customer Success
8. Long-Term Partnership

---

# Discovery Call

Objectives

- Understand the business.
- Identify pain points.
- Identify business goals.
- Determine project scope.

Deliverable

Discovery Notes

---

# Assessment

Objectives

Understand the current environment before proposing solutions.

Deliverables

- Infrastructure Assessment
- Executive Summary
- Initial Roadmap

---

# Proposal

Deliverables

- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Investment
- Assumptions

---

# Delivery

Every engagement follows the Infrastructure Modernization Framework (IMF).

---

# Customer Success

Objectives

- Validate customer satisfaction.
- Deliver final documentation.
- Identify future modernization opportunities.
