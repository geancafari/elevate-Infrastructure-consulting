# Infrastructure Documentation Playbook

Version: 1.0

Owner: Elevate Infrastructure Consulting

Status: Active

---

# Objective

Ensure every customer engagement results in complete, accurate and maintainable infrastructure documentation.

---

# Business Outcomes

- Standardized documentation
- Faster troubleshooting
- Improved knowledge transfer
- Reduced operational risk

---

- Architecture validated
- Customer review scheduled
