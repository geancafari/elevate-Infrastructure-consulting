# Infrastructure Assessment Playbook

Version: 1.0

Owner: Elevate Infrastructure Consulting

Status: Active

---

# Objective

Provide a structured methodology to assess a customer's current IT environment, identify business and technical risks, and define a prioritized modernization roadmap.

---

# Business Outcomes

- Understand the current infrastructure
- Identify technical debt
- Reduce business risk
- Prioritize modernization initiatives
- Produce executive-level recommendations

---

# Prerequisites

- Discovery meeting scheduled
- Customer sponsor identified
- Read-only administrative access approved
- Required documentation requested

---

# Phase 1 - Discovery

## Objectives

Understand the business before evaluating technology.

## Tasks

- Conduct Discovery Workshop
- Identify business objectives
- Identify pain points
- Identify critical applications
- Identify compliance requirements
- Define project scope

## Deliverables

- Discovery Notes
- Business Objectives

---

# Phase 2 - Infrastructure Assessment

## Tasks

- Inventory servers
- Inventory virtual machines
- Inventory networking
- Inventory storage
- Inventory cloud resources
- Inventory backup systems
- Inventory security controls
- Inventory documentation

## Deliverables

- Infrastructure Inventory
- Current Environment Diagram

---

# Phase 3 - Risk Assessment

## Tasks

- Identify operational risks
- Identify security risks
- Identify availability risks
- Identify backup gaps
- Identify unsupported software
- Identify single points of failure

## Deliverables

- Risk Matrix
- Executive Risk Summary

---

# Phase 4 - Recommendations

## Tasks

- Prioritize findings
- Define Quick Wins (30 days)
- Define Mid-Term Improvements (90 days)
- Define Strategic Roadmap (12–36 months)

## Deliverables

- Modernization Roadmap
- Investment Priorities

---

# Phase 5 - Executive Presentation

## Tasks

- Present findings
- Explain business impact
- Review roadmap
- Review investment priorities
- Define next engagement

## Deliverables

- Executive Presentation
- Assessment Report

---

# Phase 6 - Documentation

Update:

- Assessment Report
- Executive Summary
- Infrastructure Inventory
- Architecture Diagrams
- Case Study (if approved)

---

# Lessons Learned

Document assessment improvements for future engagements.
