# Infrastructure Modernization Assessment (IMA)

Version: 1.0

Owner: Elevate Infrastructure Consulting

---

# Executive Summary

The Infrastructure Modernization Assessment (IMA) is Elevate's flagship consulting service.

It provides organizations with a comprehensive understanding of their current IT infrastructure, identifies operational and security risks, and delivers a prioritized modernization roadmap aligned with business objectives.

Rather than immediately recommending technology purchases or cloud migrations, the IMA establishes a strategic foundation for informed decision-making.

---

# Objectives

The assessment aims to answer four key questions:

1. Where are we today?
2. What are our biggest risks?
3. Where should we be?
4. How do we get there?

Every IMA engagement includes:

- Executive Summary
- Infrastructure Inventory
- Current State Assessment
- Risk Register
- Current Architecture Diagram

- Prioritized Recommendations
- Quick Wins (30 Days)
- Strategic Roadmap (12–36 Months)
- Executive Presentation
