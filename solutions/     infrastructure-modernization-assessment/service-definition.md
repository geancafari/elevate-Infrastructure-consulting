# Infrastructure Modernization Assessment

Version: 1.0

---

## Service Overview

The Infrastructure Modernization Assessment (IMA) is Elevate's flagship consulting engagement.

The assessment provides customers with a comprehensive evaluation of their current IT infrastructure, identifies technical and operational risks, and delivers a prioritized modernization roadmap aligned with business objectives.

---

## Objectives

- Assess the current infrastructure.
- Identify business risks.
- Identify technical debt.
- Evaluate cloud readiness.
- Improve operational resilience.
- Define a modernization strategy.

---

## Target Customers

- Small Businesses
- Medium Businesses
- Organizations planning cloud adoption
- Organizations with aging infrastructure
- Companies requiring IT standardization

---

## Typical Duration

2–3 weeks

Executive Assessment
